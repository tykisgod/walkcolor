隐私政策

bAd相机 · 最后更新：2026 年 8 月 20 日

bAd相机（以下简称"本应用"）是一款胶片风格相机应用。本应用非常重视您的隐私，本政策说明我们如何处理您的信息。

我们绝不收集的

无论何种情况，本应用都不会收集或上传：

  • 您拍摄或导入的照片、Live 照片和视频本身，以及任何影像内容
  • 麦克风录制的任何音频内容
  • 姓名、邮箱、手机号等可识别您身份的信息
  • 联系人、通讯录、剪贴板内容

您拍摄的所有影像始终仅在设备本地处理和保存，从不上传至任何服务器。

相机与麦克风

本应用请求相机权限，用于以胶片预设拍摄照片和视频——这是本应用的核心功能。麦克风权限仅在拍摄 Live 照片或录制视频时用于收音。所有拍摄内容（含音频）仅保存在您的设备本地相册，不会上传。

定位（可选功能）

仅当您主动开启签名戳的「地点」文字预设时，本应用会请求"使用期间"定位权限，用于把拍摄地的城市名渲染进签名戳文字。具体而言：

  • 定位精度为公里级（城市级标注），不采集精确轨迹；
  • 坐标通过操作系统（Apple）提供的地理编码服务转换为城市名，本应用自己的服务器不接收、不存储任何位置数据；
  • 转换得到的城市名仅渲染在您自己的照片画面上，保存在您本地相册；
  • 不开启「地点」预设则完全不触发定位请求，您也可随时在系统设置中撤回授权。

相册访问

本应用会请求访问您的相册，仅用于以下目的：

  • 将拍摄或导出的照片、视频、Live 照片保存到相册
  • 导入您选择的照片进行胶片效果处理
  • 读取所选照片的拍摄信息（如拍摄地点）和 Live Photo 的视频分量，用于「地点」文字预设和 Live 导入

您选择的照片仅在设备本地处理，不会上传到任何服务器。

使用数据分析

为了改进产品体验和修复问题，本应用会通过行业通用的第三方产品分析服务收集匿名的使用数据，包括：

  • 设备型号、iOS 版本、App 版本
  • 匿名会话标识（随机 UUID，不对应您的真实身份）
  • 功能使用事件（如"完成拍摄""切换胶卷""完成导出"）及相关非敏感参数（如导出耗时等）
  • 崩溃和诊断数据：应用崩溃时的栈信息（错误类型 / 函数名），用于定位和修复 bug，不含个人识别信息

这些数据仅用于产品改进和问题排查，不会用于广告定向，也不会与任何其他方共享。相关服务器位于中国大陆境外。

后续版本将在 App 设置内提供关闭数据分析的开关；您也可以随时通过下方邮箱联系我们，要求停止对您的数据收集。

传感器使用

本应用使用设备的加速度传感器（Accelerometer），仅用于以下单一目的：

  • 摇一摇反馈：当您在使用 App 时摇动设备，App 会在屏幕上弹出确认弹窗询问您是否要提交意见反馈。该功能可在确认弹窗中点击"以后不要弹了"永久关闭。

加速度传感器的原始读数仅在设备本地内存中用于检测摇动手势，不会被记录、存储或上传。本应用不使用陀螺仪、磁力计、心率、指纹、计步器等其他类型传感器，不读取 IMEI、设备 MAC 地址、软件安装列表、通话记录、日历、短信等任何其他个人信息。

我们采取的个人信息安全保护措施

一、访问控制与最小权限原则。本应用严格遵循最小权限原则，只申请完成功能必需的系统权限（相机、麦克风、相册读写、可选的使用期间定位），不申请通讯录、电话、短信等与核心功能无关的权限。每项权限的使用目的与触发场景对用户透明，并可随时在系统设置中撤回授权。

二、数据加密与存储安全。应用数据保存在 iOS 应用私有沙箱目录，仅本应用可访问；不将任何可识别用户身份的信息保存到公共存储区域。

三、传输加密。本应用涉及的所有网络请求（匿名使用数据上报、意见反馈提交）均强制使用 HTTPS + TLS 1.2 及以上协议加密传输，不进行任何明文 HTTP 传输。

四、身份鉴别。本应用无需账号注册与登录，不存在任何需要身份鉴别的用户数据。

五、恶意代码防范。应用代码托管于受管理的代码仓库，通过自动化 CI/CD 流水线发布；引入的第三方依赖均为业界公认的合规组件（如 PostHog SDK），发布前进行静态代码分析与自动化测试。

六、安全审计与应急响应。定期审查第三方 SDK 的数据收集行为，确认与本政策的承诺一致；若发生安全事件或数据泄露，我们将在 72 小时内通过本页面发布公告，并通过下方联系邮箱向受影响用户说明处置方案。

无账号、无登录

本应用不需要注册账号或登录，不收集姓名、邮箱、手机号等可直接识别您身份的信息。

儿童隐私

本应用不针对 13 岁以下的儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的任何信息。

政策更新

如果本政策有变更，我们会在此页面发布更新版本并标注日期。

Privacy Policy

bAd Camera · Last updated: August 20, 2026

bAd Camera ("the App") is a film-style camera app. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we handle your information.

What We Never Collect

Under no circumstances does the App collect or upload:

  • Photos, Live Photos, or videos you shoot or import, or any image content
  • Any audio recorded through the microphone
  • Name, email, phone number, or any personally identifiable information
  • Contacts, address book, or clipboard content

Everything you shoot is processed and stored locally on your device and is never uploaded to any server.

Camera & Microphone

The App requests camera access to shoot photos and videos with film presets — the App's core feature. Microphone access is used only to record audio while shooting Live Photos or videos. All captured content (including audio) is saved only to your local photo library and is never uploaded.

Location (Optional Feature)

Only when you actively enable the "Location" text preset of the signature stamp, the App requests while-in-use location access to render the city name of the shooting location into the stamp text. Specifically:

  • Location accuracy is kilometer-level (city-level annotation); no precise movement tracking is collected.
  • Coordinates are converted to a city name via the operating system's (Apple's) geocoding service; our own servers never receive or store any location data.
  • The resulting city name is rendered only onto your own photo, which is saved to your local library.
  • If the "Location" preset is not enabled, no location request is ever triggered. You may revoke access at any time in system settings.

Photo Library Access

The App requests access to your photo library solely to:

  • Save captured or exported photos, videos, and Live Photos to your library
  • Import photos you select for film-effect processing
  • Read the capture metadata (such as shooting location) and the video component of Live Photos you select, for the "Location" text preset and Live import

Your photos are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded to any server.

Usage Analytics

To improve the product and diagnose issues, the App collects anonymous usage data through an industry-standard third-party product analytics service. This includes:

  • Device model, iOS version, app version
  • An anonymous session identifier (randomly generated UUID, not linked to your real identity)
  • Feature usage events (e.g. "shot captured", "film roll switched", "export completed") with non-sensitive parameters such as export duration
  • Crash and diagnostic data: stack info on app crashes (error type, function name), used for bug triage. Contains no personal identifiers.

The data is used solely for product improvement and troubleshooting, never for advertising, and is never shared with any other party. The servers used for this purpose are located outside mainland China.

A toggle to disable usage analytics will be added to the in-app Settings in a future release. In the meantime, you may email us at the address below to opt out.

Sensor Usage

The App uses the device's accelerometer for a single purpose only:

  • Shake-to-feedback: when you shake the device while using the App, a confirmation dialog appears asking whether you want to submit feedback. You may permanently disable this feature by tapping "Don't show again" in the confirmation dialog.

Raw accelerometer readings are used only in local device memory for shake gesture detection and are never logged, stored, or uploaded. The App does not use any other type of sensor (gyroscope, magnetometer, heart rate, fingerprint, pedometer, etc.), and does not read IMEI, device MAC address, installed-application list, call logs, calendar, SMS, or any other personal information.

Security Measures We Take to Protect Your Information

1. Access Control & Least Privilege. The App strictly follows the principle of least privilege, requesting only the system permissions required for core functionality (camera, microphone, photo library read/write, and optional while-in-use location). We do not request contacts, phone, SMS, or other permissions unrelated to core features. The purpose and trigger of each permission are transparent to users, who may revoke access at any time via system settings.

2. Data Encryption & Storage Security. App data is saved in the iOS app's private sandbox directory, accessible only to this app; no identifiable user information is written to public storage.

3. Encryption in Transit. All network requests (anonymous analytics, feedback submission) are forced over HTTPS + TLS 1.2 or above; no plaintext HTTP transmission is used.

4. Identity Verification. The App requires no account registration or login, so there is no user identity data requiring authentication.

5. Malicious Code Prevention. Application code is hosted in a managed repository and released via automated CI/CD pipelines; third-party dependencies are all industry-recognized components (e.g. PostHog SDK) and undergo static analysis and automated testing before release.

6. Security Audit & Incident Response. We periodically review third-party SDKs' data collection behavior to confirm compliance with this policy; in the event of a security incident or data breach, we will publish a notice on this page within 72 hours and contact affected users via the support email below.

No Accounts or Login

The App does not require registration or login, and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, or other directly identifying information.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.
